1. Who I am

Tennisnerds is a community group for tennis players in Hertfordshire and North London, run by Tariq Hassan, who is responsible for your personal data (the "data controller"). It is not a company, a club, or part of the LTA.

Contact: tennisnerds@icloud.com

2. What I collect

  • 'Join' Form: first and last name, email, phone number, WTN rating, LTA Advantage number, skill level, how often you play, where you're based, why you joined, how you heard about the group, your priorities, whether you'd host socials, your Community Alerts choice, and your Declaration and Consent.
  • Competition entries via the LTA portal: name, email, phone, WTN, and LTA Advantage number, as given on your entry.
  • Match Results Form: player and opponent names, scores, date, and venue.
  • WhatsApp: your name, number, attendance on weekend social sessions/polls, and messages in the group.
  • Social Events RSVPs: your name, email, and whether you'd like your first name hidden from the public event list.
  • Photos: occasionally at socials, posted only with your agreement.

I don't collect dates of birth, health information, or payment details.

3. Where I get your data

Mostly from you, through my join form. If you entered one of my competitions through the LTA competitions portal, I receive your details from that entry. Match results are entered by players and by me.

4. How and why I use it

  • Running the group and WhatsApp: adding you and managing the group. Basis: your consent. I add you only if you say yes.
  • Arranging matches: I give your email or phone number to your opponent, and in team leagues to the captains involved. Basis: legitimate interests, as matches can't be arranged without it.
  • Results, LTA submissions and reports: recording scores, submitting them to the LTA competitions portal, and producing match fixtures, monthly newsletters, weekly reports, and deadline reminders. Basis: legitimate interests.
  • Competition emails: If you entered one of my competitions through the LTA portal, the entry terms you accepted allow the competition organiser to contact you about the competition and future competitions and events. So I may email you when entries open for my competitions, send entry reminders, and share competition news. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Community Alerts (newsletters, socials and offers): My monthly newsletter, general announcements, and social events. I send these only if you opted in on my join form or told me you'd like them. Alerts may occasionally include discount offers. I receive no payment or products for these, and I don't share your details with anyone. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Service Emails: I send service emails about competitions you've entered, such as match reminders and deadlines. These don't depend on Community Alerts.
  • Social events: I publish the date, venue, and number of attendees of past events, and list attendees by first name unless you tick the box when you RSVP or ask me to hide you. Basis: legitimate interests, as a record of what the community does. Regular weekly sessions are arranged in WhatsApp.
  • Public leaderboard (The Nerds Race): first name, surname initial, tournaments played, current event, and points, on tennisnerds.org. The leaderboard never shows scores or contact details. If you joined through my form, my basis is your consent. If you entered through the LTA portal, my basis is legitimate interests: running a points table from competitions I organise. You can ask to be removed at any time.
  • Archived tables and Honours Board: see section 5.
  • Photos: posted only with your agreement. Tell me and I'll take them down.

5. Archived tables and Honours Board

Past standings and match results for competitions I run myself are kept on my Archived Tables pages as a record of league history. They show players' names, match scores, dates, and standings. The Honours Board lists past winners by name and season. For competitions run on the LTA portal, I link to the LTA's own pages rather than copying results. My basis is your consent if you signed up through my form, and otherwise my legitimate interest in keeping league history. You can ask me to remove or anonymise your name on these pages at any time, and I'll do it within one month. Search engines may keep copies for a short time afterwards.

6. Who sees your data

  • Tariq Hassan, who runs the group.
  • Your opponent, or team captains, to arrange fixtures.
  • Other WhatsApp members, who can see your name and number. My group rules ask members not to copy or share them.
  • The public, for the leaderboard, archived tables, Honours Board, and past social events list.
  • The LTA, which receives names, scores, venue, and date through its competitions portal. It is a separate controller with its own privacy policy.
  • Services I use: Google, Microsoft, Apple (iCloud email), WhatsApp (Meta), and GitHub Pages. Some may handle data outside the UK.

I never sell your data or give it to advertisers.

7. Storage and security

Sign-up responses go into a cloud spreadsheet and a database on my laptop, which I use for results, reports, and reminders. Access is limited to me and protected by passwords and device encryption.

8. How long I keep it

  • Contact details and sign-up answers: kept while you're active. You count as active if you're in my WhatsApp group, or have played in or entered one of my competitions in the last 24 months. Once neither is true, I delete your contact details. I review this once every couple of years, and I also delete them whenever you ask.
  • Match and results history (name, competition, season, result, points): kept long-term as a record of league history, without contact details. If you ask me to erase your data, I anonymise your name in this history rather than deleting the matches, so other players' results stay correct.
  • Social event lists: stay as event history, first names only, until you ask me to hide you.
  • Leaderboard: shows current and previous seasons only.
  • Archived tables and Honours Board: stay up as league history until you ask me to remove or anonymise your name.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your data, withdraw consent, object to being listed, or get a copy. This covers the leaderboard, archived tables, and Honours Board. Email me and I'll reply within one month. Leaving the leaderboard doesn't affect your place in the group or leagues.

10. This website

No cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses.

11. Contacts and changes

If you have any queries about this privacy policy or how I process your personal data, you may contact me at tennisnerds@icloud.com.

If you are not satisfied with how I am processing your personal data, you can make a complaint to the Information Commissioner. You can find out more about your rights under applicable data protection laws from the Information Commissioner's Office website: www.ico.org.uk.
